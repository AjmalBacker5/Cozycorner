Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Digital products, instant delivery

All products sold on The Cozy Corner are digital and delivered automatically the moment your payment is confirmed — as an instant download on the confirmation page, and again by email. Because delivery happens immediately and cannot be "returned," we're generally unable to offer refunds or cancellations once a purchase is completed and the file has been delivered.

Cancelling before payment

You can cancel an order at any point before completing payment on Cashfree's checkout page — simply close the checkout window. No charge is made unless payment is completed.

When we will make an exception

We'll investigate and make it right if:

  • You were charged but never received your file (no download, no email) — check your spam folder first, then contact us
  • You received the wrong product or a corrupted/incomplete file
  • You were charged more than once for the same order due to a technical error

In these cases, email support@thecozycorner.in with your order ID (from your confirmation email or the URL of your order-status page) and a description of the issue. We'll either resend the correct file or issue a refund to your original payment method via Cashfree, typically within 5–7 business days of approval.

What isn't covered

Refunds aren't offered for change of mind after a file has been successfully delivered, or because the guide, course, or database didn't produce the results you hoped for — see our Terms & Conditions regarding no guarantee of results.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method used at checkout, through Cashfree. We do not handle refunds via cash, bank transfer, or any method outside Cashfree's system.

Contact us

For any order issue, email support@thecozycorner.in or see our Contact page.
