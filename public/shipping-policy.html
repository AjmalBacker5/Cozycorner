Delivery Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

No physical shipping

Every product sold on The Cozy Corner — Indian Dropshipping Mastery, the AI Video Ads Course, and the Verified Sellers & Suppliers Database — is a digital file. Nothing is printed, packaged, or physically shipped, and there are no shipping charges.

How delivery works

As soon as Cashfree confirms your payment:

  • Your order-status page unlocks an instant Download now button
  • A copy of your file is automatically emailed to the address you entered at checkout

Delivery is triggered automatically by our server re-checking your payment status directly with Cashfree — there's no manual approval step, so it typically happens within seconds of payment.

If you don't receive your file

First, check your spam or promotions folder for an email from orders@thecozycorner.in. If it's genuinely missing, refresh your order-status page to re-trigger the download link, or email support@thecozycorner.in with your order ID and we'll resend it.

Delivery area

Since delivery is entirely digital, our products are available to buyers anywhere with internet access — there's no geographic restriction on delivery.

Contact us

Questions about delivery? Email support@thecozycorner.in or see our Contact page.
