Drop your real product files in this folder with these exact names
(or edit the names in lib/products.js to match your own files):

  dropshipping-module.pdf       <- the module content
  sellers-contact-list.xlsx     <- the seller contact database

These files are NEVER served publicly. The server only reads them after
Cashfree confirms a payment is PAID, either to email them to the buyer
or to stream them from /api/download.

Until you add the real files, the site still works end-to-end (payment,
webhook, order confirmation) — buyers just won't receive an attachment,
and a warning is logged on the server so you notice and can resend
manually.
